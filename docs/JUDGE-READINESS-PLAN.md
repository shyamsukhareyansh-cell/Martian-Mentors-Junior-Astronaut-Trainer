# SPACEQUEST — Judge-readiness plan
**Team:** Martian Mentors  
**Selected challenge:** Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer  
**Build:** 1.0.8

## The strongest judging story
SPACEQUEST turns a Mars outpost into a mystery the player must investigate and keep operational. The player explores rooms, connects evidence, solves applied puzzles and makes resource-allocation decisions. The central educational promise is learning why power, life support, water recovery, food production and shelter decisions interact.

## Changes in build 1.0.8
- After a successful dust-storm allocation, the game now presents a short mission debrief.
- The debrief explains the dust/solar-power relationship, essential life-support needs and resource trade-offs.
- The debrief repeats that EU/WU/FU are fictional game units, not NASA engineering values.
- A reflection prompt asks the player to think like a mission planner.

## Judging lens
- **Impact:** demonstrate what a child can learn; collect playtest observations rather than claiming learning outcomes without evidence.
- **Creativity:** show the cinematic mystery, room exploration and connected clues.
- **Validity:** cite the required NASA challenge resource, label simplified game units, and verify every scientific statement.
- **Relevance:** explicitly map power, life support, radiation protection and food production to the selected challenge.
- **Presentation:** use the short demo plan in `DEMO-SCRIPT.md`; start with gameplay, not a long explanation.
