# SPACEQUEST — Judge-readiness plan

**Team:** MARTIAN MENTORS  
**Selected challenge:** Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer  
**Build:** 1.0.17

## The strongest judging story
SPACEQUEST turns a Mars outpost into a mystery the player must investigate and keep operational. Players explore rooms, connect evidence, solve applied puzzles and make resource-allocation decisions. The educational aim is to help children understand why power, life support, water recovery, food production and shelter decisions interact.

## What the experience includes
- A story-led opening aboard ASTRAL-9, followed by exploration of a Martian outpost.
- Eleven explorable locations, with room-based interactions and connected clues.
- Puzzles, riddles and optional equation challenges.
- A simulated dust-storm resource-allocation decision followed by a learning debrief.
- An optional NASA OSDR RadLab reference activity in the Radiation Shelter.

## Judging lens
- **Impact:** Explain what a child can learn; distinguish enjoyment from demonstrated learning.
- **Creativity:** Show the cinematic mystery, room exploration and connected clues.
- **Validity:** Cite the NASA challenge resource, label simplified game units and verify scientific statements.
- **Relevance:** Connect power, life support, radiation protection and food production to the selected challenge.
- **Presentation:** Start the demo with the playable experience, then explain the educational purpose.
