# Science notes and source checklist

## Gameplay labels
- **GAME:** EU (energy units), WU (water units), FU (food units), and Shield Coverage Index are simplified fictional game quantities. They must not be presented as NASA specifications or real radiation doses.
- **NASA-BASED:** Dust reducing solar generation, resource prioritisation, life-support needs, water recovery, controlled food production, and radiation sheltering are high-level concepts adapted for a child-friendly simulation.
- **REAL:** Do not label any numeric value REAL until the team verifies it directly against a cited source and records the exact page/table/date.

No numerical engineering claims are currently attributed to NASA in this build. Validate all science and references before competition submission.

## Equation puzzle
The laboratory lock uses the standard electrical relationship **P = V × I** (power in watts = potential difference in volts × current in amperes). The example 24 V and 3 A are invented for the game puzzle; 72 W is the arithmetic result, not a claim about a NASA habitat system.

Other optional equation-vault questions use school-level relationships such as speed = distance/time, density = mass/volume, F = ma, and energy = power × time. Their example values are fictional training values, not mission specifications.
