# Child playtest protocol — ages 7–12
Use parent/guardian permission and follow school/family safeguarding rules. Do not collect children's full names, contact details, photos or identifiable recordings. Record anonymous observations only.

## Observe without coaching
- Did the player understand the opening objective?
- Could they enter the outpost and find the hub?
- Did they discover what objects could be inspected?
- Did they understand a clue or ask for a hint?
- Could they complete or meaningfully attempt the dust-storm allocation?
- Did they notice that their allocation changed power, water or food?
- Did they choose to explore another room?

## Ask after play
1. Which part did you enjoy most?
2. What was confusing or frustrating?
3. What did you learn about keeping a Mars outpost running?
4. What would you change?
