# SPACEQUEST — concise judge demo script

**Suggested length:** about 3 minutes. Show a working path rather than trying to demonstrate every feature.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
"Imagine waking up aboard a failing spacecraft. Earth is gone, the crew is missing, and a mysterious transmission is calling you towards Mars. This is SPACEQUEST: The Last Transmission."

## 0:20–0:45 — Explain the challenge fit
"Our project responds to NASA's Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge. Players explore and investigate a Mars outpost while learning about systems such as power, life support, water, food production and radiation protection."

## 0:45–1:25 — Show exploration
Show arrival at the outpost, the Central Hub and one room with an inspectable object and clue. Explain that clues connect across rooms and players investigate the environment rather than simply follow a quiz list.

## 1:25–2:15 — Show the dust-storm decision
Open the dust-storm allocation activity. Demonstrate the resource limits, submit a valid allocation and show the learning debrief. Explain that EU/WU/FU are simplified fictional game units, not NASA engineering values.

## 2:15–2:40 — Show the mystery
Show the evidence notebook and one connected clue leading towards the restricted archive. Explain how the mystery gives players a reason to keep exploring.

## 2:40–3:00 — Impact and data
"We designed SPACEQUEST to make the challenges of operating a Mars outpost approachable through exploration and decisions. Two children tried the game and gave positive feedback about the experience. Our optional Radiation Shelter activity references historical NASA OSDR RadLab observations from the International Space Station—not measurements from Mars."

**Before recording:** Only show features that work in the deployed build. Confirm the exact challenge resource listing and public link. Do not imply NASA endorsement or claim learning outcomes that were not specifically observed.
