# SPACEQUEST — concise judge demo script
Target length: about 3 minutes. Keep the demo focused; show a working path, not every feature.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
"Imagine waking up after Earth has become uninhabitable. Your ship is failing, your destination is unclear, and the last signal points to an empty Mars outpost. This is SPACEQUEST: The Last Transmission."

## 0:20–0:45 — Explain the challenge fit
"Our project responds to NASA's Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge. The player is not just answering space trivia: they investigate and operate a Mars outpost where power, life support, water, food and protection compete for limited resources."

## 0:45–1:25 — Show exploration
Show the arrival at the outpost, Central Hub, and one room with an inspectable object and a clue. Explain that clues connect across rooms and the player can explore available sectors rather than follow a quiz list.

## 1:25–2:15 — Show the dust-storm decision
Open the outpost systems and dust-storm allocation. Change the sliders, demonstrate that an over-limit allocation is rejected, then submit a valid allocation. Show the learning debrief and explain that EU/WU/FU are simplified fictional game units.

## 2:15–2:40 — Show the mystery
Show the evidence notebook and one connected clue leading towards the restricted archive. Briefly explain how the mystery gives the player a reason to keep exploring.

## 2:40–3:00 — Impact and data
"SPACEQUEST aims to help children understand mission trade-offs through exploration and decisions. We tested it with [insert actual playtest count and findings only after testing]. The NASA challenge resource used is [insert exact resource title and explain exactly how its data changes this mechanic]."
